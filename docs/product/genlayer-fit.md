# Product fit for GenLayer

## StreakPact: strong fit

StreakPact needs GenLayer precisely where a deterministic chain or ordinary API is insufficient: evaluating whether external, often human-generated evidence satisfies natural-language commitment criteria and allowing that judgment to change escrow state.

The fit is strongest when commitments use structured criteria, evidence is immutable and independently fetchable, the result affects escrow or reputation, validators compare a narrow decision surface, and low-confidence or disputed decisions have explicit state-machine paths.

The product should be positioned as **trusted, evidence-backed accountability**, not as an AI habit tracker. GenLayer’s value is transparent settlement by independent validators.

## MicroWagers: strong StudioNet demonstration fit

Prediction and resolution markets are native to GenLayer because external facts must be gathered and interpreted before escrow can settle. On StudioNet, MicroWagers is a useful demonstration of that capability without real-value launch concerns.

The product role is a StudioNet lab for private friend-group predictions using test GEN, bounded public questions, source-quality testing, and appeal-flow demonstrations.

For the current zero-value scope, completion means a recorded StudioNet deployment, a configured frontend, short demo-friendly deadlines and appeal windows, and successful creator/taker/resolve/appeal/claim acceptance tests.

## Portfolio decision

Use StreakPact as the polished flagship and MicroWagers as the focused GenLayer resolution lab. Keep separate deployment addresses and interfaces so testers always know which state machine they are exercising.
