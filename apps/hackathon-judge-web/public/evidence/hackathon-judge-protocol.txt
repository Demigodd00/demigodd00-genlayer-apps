VERIFIABLE IMPLEMENTATION
The contract captures each public evidence page with gl.nondet.web.render(). The leader and validators must agree on the exact normalized snapshot and its SHA-256 digest before either is stored. A later judgment reads only that immutable snapshot.

Validators independently interpret the on-chain prose rulebook and rubric. Consensus compares eligibility and the 20-point score band exactly, permits a bounded confidence difference, and deliberately excludes free-form rationale wording. Appeals may add one independently captured snapshot. Ranking, prize credit, withdrawal, refunds, and address-based win credentials are deterministic.

LIVENESS AND TESTS
Any account may evaluate, resolve an appeal, finalize, or apply the 24-hour timeout fallback. A timed-out unresolved judgment becomes INCONCLUSIVE so no submission can lock the event or prize forever. GenVM lint passes, 20 direct tests cover lifecycle and adversarial cases, the frontend check passes, and the production dependency audit reports no known vulnerabilities.
